RESEARCH & ORDERING

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about research products, testing, fulfillment, and what happens after an order request is submitted.

What does “research use only” mean?

Products offered through this catalog are sold as research materials for laboratory and analytical use. They are not represented as approved medicines, treatments, or consumer-use products.

Are these products intended for human or veterinary use?

No. Products in the research catalog are not offered for human or veterinary use, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of disease.

How can I review product testing?

When a batch Certificate of Analysis is available in our system, a View COA link appears with the product or strength. COAs are tied to the available batch data rather than being treated as a general claim about every product.

Why do we blind test with mass spectrometry labs when we can?

When practical, we submit samples under a blind or generic identifier so the testing lab is evaluating the material in the vial rather than relying on a product name, vendor label, or expectation about what it should contain. We prefer independent labs that can use mass spectrometry for identity testing when that method is available, along with appropriate chromatographic testing for purity and content. This helps reduce confirmation bias and gives us a stronger independent check of what the sample actually is. The exact test method can vary by product and laboratory, so the individual COA is the source for the methods used on that specific sample.

What do “In Stock” and “Coming Soon” mean?

In Stock reflects inventory currently recorded as available. Coming Soon identifies inventory already marked as inbound. Availability is confirmed again before payment is requested.

What preparation options are available?

Eligible research products can be requested in their original sealed/unprepared format, pre-mixed before fulfillment, or pre-mixed and prepared in a Disposable Research Pen. The available option and its fee are shown in checkout before the order request is submitted.

How does shipping and pickup work?

Retail checkout currently offers $10 flat-rate shipping or free pickup. The selected fulfillment method is included in the order summary before submission.

What happens after I submit an order request?

The site generates a unique 5-digit order reference and locks the submitted form. Keep that number. We use it to match your request when availability is confirmed and the payment link is sent by email or text.

Can I change an order after it is submitted?

The submitted form cannot be edited or submitted again from the confirmation screen. If something needs to change, reference the 5-digit order number when you contact us so the correct request can be identified.

How should research products be stored?

Storage requirements can differ by material and format. Follow the product label, batch documentation, and any storage information provided with the specific research material rather than assuming one storage method applies to every product.
